How to read this. "Award value" is the award's total amount as reported to USAspending, not money left to spend. "Ends" is the current period-of-performance end date. Coverage: the expiring contracts found among the top of contracts by award amount, so the true count is higher. Set-aside type is not in this USAspending pull, so there is no set-aside filter.
